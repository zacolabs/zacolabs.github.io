Dayline Privacy Policy

한국어 · English

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Zaco Labs ("the Company") values your privacy and complies with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, stored, and destroyed within the Dayline service ("the Service") provided by the Company. This policy applies to both the iOS and Android apps.

1. Personal Information We Collect

The Company provides the Service without an account sign-up process, and the app does not ask you to enter information that directly identifies you, such as your name or phone number. The Company runs no server and neither collects nor receives your movement record. The following information is collected in the course of providing the Service:

  • Advertising identifier (behavioral information): IDFA on iOS and the Advertising ID (ADID) on Android (see Section 3)
  • Device information needed to serve ads: device model, OS version, app version, your IP address and the approximate region derived from it, and which ads were seen or tapped. The advertising SDK included in the app sends this directly to Google. (see Section 3)
  • When you contact us: the email address you send from and the content of your inquiry

The following information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to the Company or to third parties.

  • Movement record: location (coordinates) and time, the path you travel, distance and duration, places where you stayed, and how you travelled (walking, running, cycling, driving)
  • Motion and activity information from your device (including steps), used to tell travel modes apart
  • App settings: language, theme, distance unit, and the like

The Company does not include usage-analytics or crash-diagnostics tools in the app.

2. Purposes of Collection and Use

  • Recording and showing your day's path, distance, and travel modes (processed only on the device)
  • Providing personalized advertising, measuring ad performance, and preventing ad fraud
  • Responding to customer inquiries

3. Collection and Use of Advertising Identifiers (Behavioral Information)

To provide the Service free of charge, the Company uses a third-party advertising platform, and an advertising identifier may be collected in this process. Your movement record is not used for ads, and the app does not pass it to the advertising platform.

  1. Behavioral information collected
    • iOS: IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers)
    • Android: Advertising ID (ADID, Google Advertising ID)
  2. Purposes of collection and use
    • Providing interest-based personalized (targeted) advertising
    • Analyzing ad impression and click performance and preventing ad fraud (invalid traffic)
  3. Collection method and third-party service
    • Collected and transmitted automatically through the mobile advertising SDK included in the app.
    • Service: Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google's data processing is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  4. Your right to opt out and how to do so
    You can refuse the collection of identifiers for personalized advertising in the following ways. Even if you opt out, non-personalized (general) ads may still be shown.
    • iOS: Decline the tracking request shown when you first launch the app, or go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" or turn off tracking for the specific app.
    • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads and select "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
    • Users in regions where consent is required for advertising, such as the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, can make a choice on the consent screen shown when the app is first launched, and can change it at any time from Settings > Ad privacy in the app.

4. Processing of Location Information and Your Movement Record

  • Location and motion information is used only to create your movement record. It is processed on the device and stored only on the device, and is not transmitted to the Company's servers or to third parties.
  • On screens that show a map, the map provider (Apple Maps on iOS, Google Maps on Android) downloads map data for the area on screen from its own servers. The map provider's data processing is subject to the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Exporting a GPX file and sharing an image happen only when you choose to, and go only where you send them.

5. Retention and Use Period

The Company does not hold your movement record. Information stored only on the device (Section 1) stays on the device until you delete it. You can remove everything with Settings > Delete all records in the app, and deleting the app removes it from the device as well. Because the Company does not hold your records, it cannot delete or restore them for you.

The email address and inquiry content received through a customer inquiry are destroyed without delay once the inquiry has been handled. However, where retention is required under applicable laws, they are retained for the applicable period.

The retention period of information collected by Google, such as the advertising identifier, follows Google's data retention policy.

6. Provision to Third Parties

The Company does not provide your personal information to third parties beyond the scope specified in this policy, except where required under the provisions of law or where an investigative agency so requests for investigative purposes in accordance with the procedures and methods prescribed by law.

7. Entrustment of Personal Information Processing

The Company entrusts the following personal information processing tasks for the smooth provision of the Service:

  • Google LLC: personalized advertising and ad consent management (Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform)

8. Transfer of Personal Information Abroad

The Company transfers personal information abroad as follows for advertising.

  • Recipient: Google LLC (contact: Google privacy inquiries)
  • Destination countries: the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers
  • Items transferred: advertising identifier, device model, OS version, app version, IP address, and ad impression and click records
  • Timing and method of transfer: sent over the network from time to time through the SDK included in the app while you use it
  • Purposes of use and retention period: the purposes in Section 3, for the period under Google's data retention policy
  • How to refuse and its effect: you can refuse the transfer of the advertising identifier for personalized advertising through the settings in Section 3. Even if you refuse, the information needed to show non-personalized ads is transmitted while you use the app.

9. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of the processing of your personal information at any time. Your movement record exists only on your device, so you can view it directly in the app, export it with Settings > Export records (GPX), or delete it with Settings > Delete all records. For any other request, email the address in Section 13 and the Company will take action without delay in accordance with applicable laws.

10. Destruction of Personal Information

Information in electronic file form is permanently deleted using a method that prevents recovery or reproduction, and information printed on paper is shredded or incinerated.

11. App Permissions

The app uses device permissions for the features below. Even if you do not allow an optional permission, you can use the Service apart from that feature.

  • Location (required): used to record the path you travel. Recording without opening the app needs "Always".
  • Motion & Fitness (iOS, optional): used to tell walking, running, cycling, and driving apart.
  • Add to Photos (iOS, optional): used only when you save the picture of a day to your photo library. The app does not read your photos.
  • Allow app tracking (iOS, optional): used for personalized advertising.

You can change or withdraw permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

12. Measures to Ensure Security

The Company does not keep your movement record on any server. Records are stored on your device and protected by the device's own security features, such as the device lock. For information received through customer inquiries, the Company implements administrative and technical protective measures, including access-rights management.
