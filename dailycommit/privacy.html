Daily Commit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Daily Commit is a macOS app used by its developer, Zaco Labs, to manage the Threads, Instagram and YouTube accounts of Daily Commit (@dailycommit.dev). It is not offered to other users.

What the app accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app publishes posts to the owner's own accounts and reads their basic profile. To suggest replies, it searches public Threads posts for a few keywords about Google Play's closed test and reads each post's text, the author's username, the post link and the time it was posted.

How it is used

Search results are used only to show the account owner posts they may want to reply to, each with a suggested reply. The app never posts replies to other people's posts: the owner reads the post, edits the reply if needed and posts it by hand, saying that they make the service they mention.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the account owner's own Mac. There is no server. Fetched posts are deleted after 60 days. To write a suggested reply, the post text is sent to the AI model the owner uses (Anthropic Claude or OpenAI Codex). Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising or analytics.

Your choices

If you wrote a post the app fetched and want it removed from the app's records before the 60 days pass, write to the address below with the post's link.
