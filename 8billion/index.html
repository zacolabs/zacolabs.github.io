05 / What remains

A life ends.
Its story stays.

Past lives gather in your archive and collection. Share a birth card as an image and look back on the lives you have lived. What remains is no total score, but what that person held on to, and the moments that passed them by.

Before your first life

Is it free to play?

Yes. Open new worlds by watching a short video, or buy Premium once to open them without ads.

Do I need an account?

There is no account and no sign-in. Your lives are saved on your device.

Can I change the pace or the wording of events?

You can adjust the speed and choose gentler wording for heavy events.

Are the characters real people?

Characters and families are generated for the game. Country statistics set the scene for a life; they do not predict a real person’s life.
